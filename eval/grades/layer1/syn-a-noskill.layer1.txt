# Metric definition: PixelPress Pro mobile trial conversion **Headline number:** **14.3% of PixelPress Pro mobile trial users converted to paid** for the March cohort. In plain language, this is the share of people who started a PixelPress Pro free trial on iOS or Android during the March cohort window and then had a first positive-dollar charge soon enough to count as a conversion. ``` March mobile trial starters 48,210 users | | first paid charge within the measured conversion window v Converted to paid 6,894 users Conversion rate = 6,894 / 48,210 = 14.30% ``` ## How to interpret it A conversion rate of **14.3%** means that about **14 out of every 100** users who entered the measured free-trial cohort became paying subscribers under the implemented counting rules. This is a cohort metric, not a same-day sales metric. Users enter the denominator when they start a trial. They enter the numerator only if a qualifying first paid charge is observed within the conversion window after their own trial start date.
