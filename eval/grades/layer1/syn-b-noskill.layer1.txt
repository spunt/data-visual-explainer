# Metric definition: Home v2 active-days lift **Headline number:** users assigned to **Home v2** were active **0.21 more days** during the first 28 days after assignment than users assigned to the current home experience. In plain language, this metric asks: **after someone enters the experiment, on how many different days do they come back and do anything in the app?** The headline compares that average count between the randomized treatment and control groups. ```text Average active days in the 28-day follow-up window Control 6.42 days |█████████████████████████ Home v2 6.63 days |██████████████████████████ Difference +0.21 days |█ Interpretation: about one extra active day for every 5 treatment users over their first 28 days after assignment. ``` ## One-sentence definition **Active days per user** is the number of distinct calendar days on which a user has at least one app activity event during days 1 through 28 after experiment assignment, averaged across users in an experiment arm. The experiment's reported treatment effect is: ```text average active days in treatment - average active days in control = 6.63 - 6.42 = +0.21 active days per user ``` The reported relative lift is approximately: ```text 0.21 / 6.42 = 3.3% ```
