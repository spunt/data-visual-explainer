What share of people who started a PixelPress Pro mobile free trial in the March cohort became paying users?
14.3%
mobile trial users who converted to paid
Among users whose first PixelPress Pro mobile trial started from 2 to 9 March 2026, 14.3% had a positive first charge from trial day 0 through day 14.
6,894 of 48,210 users in the implemented March cohort had a qualifying charge.
≈About 143 in every 1,000 trial users had a positive first charge in the implemented window.        
What kind of claim: Describes a level. This number describes what happened in one group. It does not compare groups or explain why.
        Divide users with a qualifying charge by all users in the implemented trial cohort.
✓ Safe to say
"In the implemented March mobile cohort, 14.3% of PixelPress Pro trial users converted to paid."
✗ Not this
"The new onboarding caused a 1.4-point lift," or "this is a 30-day conversion rate."      
! Before you repeat it
