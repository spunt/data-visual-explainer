# Readout: PixelPress Pro trial conversion (March)

**14.3% of trial users converted to paid**, up from 12.9% for the February cohort.

The new onboarding flow shipped on 2026-03-01, so the March cohort is the first to see it.
The new onboarding is driving a 1.4-point lift in conversion. We recommend rolling it out
to web.

Definition: users who started a trial in the cohort week and paid within 30 days.
